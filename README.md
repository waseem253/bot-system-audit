## What this audit answers

For a team that already runs automation bots and wants to scale further:

- Where are the workflow gaps, silent failures and bot-to-bot handoff losses?
- Which reported metrics are wrong, and by how much?
- Where is lead data leaking or duplicating?
- What can a prompt-injection or unbounded generation actually trigger?
- What must be fixed **before** more automation is safe to add?

Every finding ships with reproduced evidence, business impact, a recommended fix, an effort estimate and a severity (Critical / High / Medium / Low) — then a roadmap sequenced quick-wins-first.

## Why an external audit

Internal teams stop seeing the seams between bots that are each individually "fine". This framework is deliberately adversarial and evidence-based: I drive the live system through the failure paths rather than theorising them.
